Artists talk of Lada Nakonechna in the frame of the exhibition *Zeichnung. Edee-Geste-Raum.*<br>
<br>
Museum unter Tage, Bochum<br>
06.03.2025, 18.00 <br>
<br>

During the talk Lada Nakonechna will present and discuss her artworks and approaches to dealing with the medium of drawing. Working with a wide range of techniques, Nakonechna experiments with the applications of drawing and does not limit it to its representational function. Interested in public perception, she plays with viewpoints by incorporating them into her artworks. Nakonechna will also discuss the drawings "Trap" and "Investigation of Manipulation Mechanisms", which are now on view in the exhibition at MUT. The audience will have the opportunity to learn about the ideas and processes behind the final artworks presented.
