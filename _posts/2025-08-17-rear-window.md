Berlin Art Week Special:<br>
Artist Talk Lada Nakonechna GROUND LEVEL<br>
Thursday, September 11, 2025, 6 p.m.<br>
Kassel-based Ukrainian artist Lada Nakonechna will discuss her work BELOW GROUND LEVEL.<br>
In her seemingly brittle drawings and collages depicting fragmented landscapes and architecture, Lada Nakonechna seeks a visual language of condensation to confront the drastic experiences of war and destruction.<br>
Lecture in English, presented by Ina Bierstedt, artist and curator<br><br>
