Lada Nakonechna interviewed HFBK students Alisa Sizykh, Daria Maiier, Lesia Hudz, and Nadiia Mykhailiuk for **Lerchenfeld #80**, a printed magazine by HFBK Hamburg.
<br><br>
In 2022, HFBK Hamburg welcomed Ukrainian art students fleeing the Russian invasion, offering them the opportunity to continue their studies in Hamburg. In 2026, most of the students earned their bachelor's degrees and presented their graduation projects. Lada Nakonechna spoke to them.
