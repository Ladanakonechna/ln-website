Exhibition **Distant Tolerable Murder**<br>
co-curated by Lada Nakonechna and Anna Laźar<br>
is finalist of The House of Europe Award<br>
Nomination:<br>
Best International Cultural Cooperation
<br><br>
The House of Europe Award honours individuals and organisations
that are driving Ukraine forward through partnership with the EU and the UK.<br>
[More info](https://award.houseofeurope.org.ua/)
<br>
<br>
Distant Tolerable Murder is an exhibition in Berlin's urban space in September 2022<br>
co-curated by Lada Nakonechna and Anna Laźar<br>
supported by the Adam Mickiewicz Institute, Warsaw<br>
[More info](https://culture.pl/en/event/distant-tolerable-murder-polish-ukrainian-exhibition-in-the-centre-of-berlin)
<br><br>
The award ceremony: 7.05.2026<br>
